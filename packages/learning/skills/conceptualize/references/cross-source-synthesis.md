# Cross-Source Concept Synthesis

Use this reference to derive one concept from variation across sources or cases.

## Comparison Before Abstraction

Construct the evidence comparison before choosing a name:

```text
Context | Local expression | Mechanism or relation | Example | Boundary | Contradiction
```

Do not count repeated wording as independent support. Prefer differences in
source, domain, representation, or use context that put pressure on the same
candidate mechanism.

## Derivation

1. Preserve source claims, interpretations, and hypotheses as different evidence
   levels.
2. Identify what each source-local example lets a learner predict, decide, or
   correct.
3. Translate local nouns into roles only when their relations remain intact.
4. Ask what remains true when language, library, chapter, or project changes.
5. Prefer operations over categories, relations over objects, and constraints
   over slogans.
6. Preserve canonical technical terms as anchors when removing them would lose
   precision.
7. State one practical question the candidate answers.

## Independence Tests

A candidate is not yet source-independent when:

- its definition requires a chapter title, project name, or local example;
- changing the implementation removes the whole concept;
- examples share only vocabulary or topic;
- the candidate merely says a property is important;
- evidence from another context changes the mechanism rather than instantiating
  it.

One source plus a transfer case may support a provisional candidate. Mark the
independence claim as limited until another independent source or observation
applies pressure.

## Provenance Without Dependence

Keep a compact evidence trail in the proposal:

```text
Source or case:
What it contributed:
Evidence level:
How it supports, challenges, or limits the candidate:
```

The concept definition must stand without this trail, but the confidence claim
must not.

## Stop

Stop when one source-independent mechanism explains the compared cases, local
anchors remain available, and contradictions are visible. If no shared
mechanism survives, return “no concept yet” with the discriminating differences.
